Claude subscription quota exhaustion ("You've hit your session limit · resets 9:20am (UTC)") now PARKS the ticket until the stated reset instead of blocking it or silently re-running on a paid OpenRouter level; the paid fallback is opt-in via `claude_exhaustion_paid_fallback` (default off) and the no-hint park interval is `claude_usage_exhausted_retry_seconds`. Exhaustion is also a provider failure, so no spec fingerprint is recorded.
