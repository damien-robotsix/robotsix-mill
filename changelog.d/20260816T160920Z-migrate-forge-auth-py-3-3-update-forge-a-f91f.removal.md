Migrated `forge/auth.py` to consume `robotsix-github-auth`, eliminating ~150 lines of duplicated JWT/installation-token logic. The internal `_build_app_jwt()`, `_mint_installation_token()` functions, and `GitHubAppNotInstalledError` exception are removed in favour of the library's `mint_installation_token()`, structured exception hierarchy (`GithubAuthError`, `TokenMintError`), and cache management (`invalidate_token_cache()`, `clear_token_cache()`). `gitlab_token()` remains mill-specific.
