`load_and_run_agent` no longer lets llmio's tier-fallback loop move a Claude-backed (subscription) run onto a keyed OpenRouter tier when the Claude level fails — seen live right after #3054 as `auto-approve triage: level2 failed with ClaudeSDKUsageExhaustedError — falling back to level3`. With `claude_exhaustion_paid_fallback` off (default) a Claude start fails through to the worker, which parks the ticket until the quota resets; keyed starts keep the fallback.
