The CI_FAILURE diagnostic event emitter in the ci_fix stage now falls back to `ticket.board_id` when `ctx.repo_config` is `None` or has no `board_id`.  Previously a missing or unresolvable repo config silently skipped the event, starving the recurring-category → auto-fix-proposal pipeline of input.  Successful and skipped emissions are now logged at info / warning level for observability.  Three regression tests cover the happy path, the `repo_config=None` fallback, and the non-emission on pending CI.
